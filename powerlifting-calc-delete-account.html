Account Deletion Request - BarbellCalc

How to request account deletion

If you would like to permanently delete your BarbellCalc account and all associated cloud-synced workout data, please send an email request to our support team.

Step 1: Send an email to:

anthony.barnes@abarnes.app

Step 2: Include the following in your email:

  • The subject line: "BarbellCalc Account Deletion Request"
  • The exact email address associated with your BarbellCalc / Google login.

We will process your request and permanently delete your Firebase authentication account and all associated Google Firestore database records within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

What data is deleted?

  • Your email address and Google Authentication User ID.
  • All cloud-synced workout logs, lift numbers, and app settings stored in our database.

Note: Deleting your cloud account will not delete the local data stored physically on your device. If you wish to delete your local data, simply uninstall the BarbellCalc app from your phone.
